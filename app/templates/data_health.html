Curation

Take the list away, and bring decisions back.

The queue leaves as a spreadsheet, because that is what a person can actually work through. One row per product, heaviest first, with the three best candidate reference products already found — so you pick rather than search. Fill in the blue columns and send it back.

Every answer becomes a rule, not a number. A decision records that this product is that reference product; the footprint is recalculated from it, so it keeps improving as the sources do. And where the product has a barcode the decision is stored against that, which means it applies to the same item from any wholesaler — for every client, once.

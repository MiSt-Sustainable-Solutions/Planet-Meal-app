The decision
{% if d %}

A person decided this.

Somebody opened a review sheet, read this product’s name, and said what it is. That answer is stored as a rule, not a number: it records which reference food this product IS, and the footprint is recalculated from that every time — so it improves when the reference data does, and no figure anywhere was typed in by hand.

There are four answers a reviewer can give. pin names one exact reference food. group names a kind of food, when the exact one is not knowable. nonfood takes it out of the food totals altogether. no records that none of the suggestions fitted, so it is not asked again. A decision outranks every automatic rule in the system, for every client, which is why it is the one thing worth being able to take back.

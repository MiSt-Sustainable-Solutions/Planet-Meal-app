Three different kinds of grouping, and why that is confusing
Sligro category ZUIVEL HOUDBAAR — the wholesaler’s own words, on their invoice. We read it, we do not choose it.
EAT-Lancet bucket dairy, white_meat, red_meat — 17 of them. This is what the dashboard’s EAT-Lancet score is built on, and its average is a product’s last resort for a footprint.
Food group Milk and milk products, Bread — 27 of them. These are the answers a reviewer can pick in the review sheet when they know the kind of thing but not the exact product.

A product has all three at once, and they will not look like each other. Whole milk is ZUIVEL HOUDBAAR to Sligro, dairy to EAT-Lancet, and would be Milk and milk products if somebody grouped it by hand.
